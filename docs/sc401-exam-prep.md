# SC-401 Exam Prep for Android — Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

BluePrint Studio · 2026-11-07 · Android app: `com.musa.exam.sc401`

## English

**Practice and local storage.** You can use SC-401 Exam Prep without creating an account. The question bank is bundled with the app. Your answers, results, practice history, unfinished session, preferences and AI consent and usage settings are stored in the app's storage. This version does not send learning progress to Firestore, retrieve a remote question bank or initiate purchases.

**App integrity.** At startup the app initializes Google Firebase. Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to help verify the app and device. This can involve app or installation identifiers, network information such as your IP address, SDK information and integrity tokens, even when you do not ask the AI tutor a question. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Optional AI tutor.** Before using the tutor, you must confirm that you are at least 18 and agree to send data to Google. When you ask a question, the app sends your message, the exam question, its answer choices, correct answer and explanation, the response language and recent chat history through Firebase AI Logic to Google Gemini to generate a reply. Avoid entering personal or confidential information. You can withdraw consent in the app to stop future AI requests. Google's handling and retention of prompts and replies depend on the service and applicable terms, including possible human review and product improvement under the unpaid-service terms. See the [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Withdrawing consent does not erase information already processed by Google.

**Email and feedback.** If you report a question, the app prepares an email with the question ID, category, question, selected answer and correct answer. You choose whether to send it. If the mail app cannot be opened, the report may be copied to the clipboard. If you contact us, BluePrint Studio uses your email address and message to handle the request and retains them for as long as reasonably needed for that purpose.

**Retention, security and deletion.** App data remains on the device until it is replaced, reset or cleared. You can clear the app's storage in Android Settings. Android backup or device transfer may preserve a copy, depending on your device settings. Clearing local data does not delete backups, sent emails or records retained by service providers. Firebase requests use encrypted connections. To ask about privacy or request deletion of information you sent directly to BluePrint Studio, email [musa561128@gmail.com](mailto:musa561128@gmail.com).
