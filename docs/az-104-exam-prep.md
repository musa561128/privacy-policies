# Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

**Effective Date / 発効日:** August 2, 2026

## English

This Privacy Policy applies to the AZ-104 Exam Prep mobile application (the "Application") for Android and iOS devices. The Application is provided by the developer (the "Service Provider") as a freemium service and is intended for use "AS IS".

### Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect information that directly identifies a user, such as a name, postal address, phone number, or email address.

The Application may process the following information to provide its features:

* Learning progress, quiz results, selected answers, weak-area information, and app settings
* Purchase status required to unlock Premium features
* AI feature usage counts stored on the device
* Text entered by the user and question context sent when the user invokes AI-powered explanations or chat
* Device and technical information required by Firebase and its app-attestation services, such as app version, operating system, device integrity signals, and diagnostic information

Learning progress, quiz results, selected answers, weak-area information, app settings, and AI usage counts are stored locally on the user's device. The Application downloads exam questions and categories from Firebase Cloud Firestore when available, but it does not upload the user's learning progress or quiz results to Firestore. The Service Provider does not sell personal data and does not collect precise location information.

### Advertising

The Application does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

### Third-Party Services

The Application uses or may use the following third-party services. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies:

* [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (Android platform services)
* [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice) (Android in-app purchases)
* [Apple App Store / In-App Purchase](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (iOS platform services and in-app purchases)
* [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) (exam-content delivery, App Check, and AI functionality)
* [Google AI / Gemini](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (AI-powered explanations and chat through Firebase AI Logic)

When the user invokes an AI-powered feature, the text entered by the user and the question context necessary to generate a response are sent through Firebase AI Logic to Google AI / Gemini. Users should not enter personal, confidential, or sensitive information into AI-powered features.

Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity on Android and App Attest on iOS to help confirm that requests originate from the genuine Application. These services may process device or app integrity signals; the Service Provider does not use them to identify users personally.

For in-app purchases, the Service Provider does not directly collect or store payment card information. Payment information is processed by Google or Apple, depending on the platform used.

### Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally in the Application remains on the user's device until the user resets the relevant app data or uninstalls the Application. Users can remove locally stored learning data by using the Application's reset function where available, clearing the Application's data on Android, or uninstalling the Application.

AI prompts and related context are processed by third-party services subject to their applicable retention and privacy terms. If the user contacts the Service Provider by email, the Service Provider may retain the contact information and message contents for the time reasonably necessary to respond and maintain support records.

To request deletion of data provided directly to the Service Provider, please use the contact information below. The Service Provider will respond within a reasonable time.

### Opt-Out Rights

Users can stop the Application from processing information by uninstalling it. Users may avoid optional AI-powered features if they do not want text or question context processed by third-party AI services.

### Children

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

If the Service Provider becomes aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, the Service Provider will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian who believes that a child has provided personal information should contact the Service Provider.

### Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

### Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be communicated by updating this page. Users are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

### Contact Us

For questions about privacy or the Service Provider's practices, contact musa561128@gmail.com.

本アプリはユーザー登録を必要とせず、氏名、住所、電話番号、メールアドレスなど、ユーザーを直接特定する情報を意図的に収集しません。

学習進捗、クイズ結果、選択した回答、弱点情報、アプリ設定、AI利用回数はユーザーの端末内に保存されます。本アプリは利用可能な場合にFirebase Cloud Firestoreから試験問題とカテゴリを取得しますが、ユーザーの学習進捗やクイズ結果をFirestoreへアップロードしません。サービス提供者は個人データを販売せず、正確な位置情報を収集しません。

### 第三者サービス

本アプリは以下の第三者サービスを利用する、または利用する場合があります。各サービスは、それぞれのプライバシーポリシーに従って情報を処理する場合があります。

* [Google Play 開発者サービス](https://policies.google.com/privacy)（Androidのプラットフォーム機能）
* [Google Play Billing](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice)（Androidのアプリ内購入）
* [Apple App Store / In-App Purchase](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)（iOSのプラットフォーム機能およびアプリ内購入）
* [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)（試験コンテンツ配信、App Check、AI機能）
* [Google AI / Gemini](https://policies.google.com/privacy)（Firebase AI Logicを通じたAI解説およびAIチャット）

ユーザーがAI機能を利用した場合、ユーザーが入力したテキストおよび回答生成に必要な問題の文脈情報が、Firebase AI Logicを通じてGoogle AI / Geminiへ送信されます。AI機能には、個人情報、機密情報、またはセンシティブな情報を入力しないでください。

Firebase App Checkは、AndroidではGoogle Play Integrity、iOSではApp Attestを使用して、正規の本アプリからのリクエストであることを確認します。これらのサービスは端末またはアプリの完全性シグナルを処理する場合がありますが、サービス提供者はユーザーを個人として特定する目的には使用しません。

アプリ内購入に関して、サービス提供者は支払いカード情報を直接収集または保存しません。支払い情報は、利用するプラットフォームに応じてGoogleまたはAppleにより処理されます。

本アプリ内にローカル保存されたデータは、ユーザーが該当するアプリデータをリセットするか、本アプリをアンインストールするまで端末内に保持されます。利用可能な場合は本アプリのリセット機能、Androidのアプリデータ消去、または本アプリのアンインストールにより、ローカル保存された学習データを削除できます。

AIへの入力内容と関連する文脈情報は、適用される第三者サービスの保持条件およびプライバシー条件に従って処理されます。ユーザーがメールでサービス提供者に問い合わせた場合、問い合わせへの対応およびサポート記録の維持に合理的に必要な期間、連絡先情報および問い合わせ内容を保持する場合があります。

サービス提供者に直接提供したデータの削除を希望する場合は、下記のお問い合わせ先までご連絡ください。サービス提供者は合理的な期間内に対応します。
